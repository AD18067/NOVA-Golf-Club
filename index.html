Our mission

Everyone gets one first swing that does not count

Our mission is to make golf genuinely accessible to every Nova SBE student, regardless of experience, budget or background. Golf is one of the most useful rooms in business and one of the hardest for a student to get into: a round costs more than a night out, the equipment costs more than a semester of textbooks, and the etiquette is unwritten, which means the only way to learn it is to already know someone who plays.

We exist to remove those barriers. We negotiate rates, we provide clubs, and we run formats where a beginner and a single-figure player can finish the same afternoon without either of them wishing they had not come.

Our vision is a club where picking up a club for the first time and representing Nova SBE competitively both belong on the same calendar. What that buys a student is not a hobby. It is the ability to accept an invitation. When a firm suggests a round, you can say yes, and you know what to do when you get there.

Formats

What actually happens at each kind of event

Clinic

Two hours, no pressure

A coach, a bucket of balls and borrowed clubs. You stand on a range, not a course, so nobody is waiting behind you. This is where most of our members start.

Scramble

Teams of four, one ball

Everyone hits, the team plays the best shot, everyone plays again from there. A beginner in the group is an asset, because they only need one good shot a hole.

Simulator

Indoors and scored for you

A screen, a mat and a launch monitor. Cheap, weatherproof, and the easiest way to run something competitive with no handicaps involved.

Stroke play

Everyone counts their own shots

The real thing, and what the competitive squad plays. We only use it for tournaments, because it takes five hours and rewards experience heavily.

Why the scramble format matters to you

In a scramble the team plays the best shot each time. It means a student who has never played can be in your group without slowing the day down, and it means your representative is not stuck watching someone struggle for four hours. Everybody talks the whole way round, which is the entire point.

How to start

Your first round, step by step

This is the actual process at Centro Nacional de Formação de Golf Jamor, our partner course, not a rough guide.

01

Verify

Fill in the form on this site with your Nova email and student ID. We check automatically that it is a real @novasbe.pt address before anything is issued.

02

Get your code

A confirmation email arrives with a single-use code. It expires the moment it is used, so a screenshot from a friend will not work — each student verifies their own.

03

Show up and book

Bring the code and your student ID to Centro Nacional de Formação de Golf Jamor. €19 for a round, €10 for the driving range, any time, in or out of an NGC event. €15 for the beginner class: a putting lesson, driving range practice and a basket of balls. Clubs are provided for the beginner class only; accessible club rental is available otherwise. Once a booking is confirmed it is not refundable, so check the date before you send it.

Glossary

Words you will hear

Par
The number of shots a good player should take on a hole. Usually three, four or five.
Birdie and bogey
One shot under par, and one shot over. Bogeys are far more common.
Tee
Both the peg you rest the ball on and the area you start each hole from.
Green
The very short grass around the hole, where you putt.
Fairway
The mown strip between tee and green. Where you are aiming.
Rough
The longer grass either side. Where the ball actually goes.
Bunker
A sand hazard. Rake it after you play out of it.
Scramble
A team format where everyone plays from the best shot in the group.
Stroke play
Everyone counts every shot. The standard tournament format.
Flight
A group going round together, finishing together. You will hear it called a fourball out on the course.
The turn
The break between the ninth and tenth holes. Where the conversation happens.
Honour
The right to tee off first, held by whoever scored best on the previous hole.

Accessibility

We want this site to be usable for everyone, and we are still working on it.

NOVA Golf Club is a small, volunteer-run student club, and this site is maintained by students alongside their studies. We have tried to build it with accessibility in mind: clear text contrast, readable type sizes, a layout that works on mobile as well as desktop, and semantic structure so screen readers can navigate it.

This is not a formal accessibility audit, and there will be gaps we have not caught. If something on this site is hard to use, hard to read, or does not work with the assistive technology you use, please tell us. We will do our best to fix it, and student volunteer capacity means fixes may take time rather than happening immediately.

Report an accessibility issue to golf.club@novasbe.pt, with a description of the problem and, if possible, which page it is on and what device or software you were using.
